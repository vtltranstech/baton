## Story
As a Billing Clerk, I want overdue invoices flagged every morning so that I can follow up the same day.

## Acceptance criteria

- AC2: Given an invoice paid yesterday, when the job runs, then it is not flagged.
- AC3: Given an overdue invoice, when the job runs twice, then the customer gets one notice in notices.
