# S-120 wireframe options

| Option | First decision | Above the fold | Follows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Option 1 | is the total right | header, lines, then totals | new: no screen of record yet |
| Option 2 (recommended) | can I send it | header, totals, send; lines below | new |
| Option 3 | which line is wrong | lines first, totals in a side column | new |

Option 2 puts the clerk's first decision (send or not) above the fold, with the total and its status beside the action; the lines that explain the total follow.
