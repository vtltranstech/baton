Pricing rules

This page is the export of the Confluence page the pricing team keeps.

Surcharges

Orders shipped to the EU must carry a surcharge when the regionCode starts with EU. Rush orders are charged a fee.

  • A wholesale order never pays the EU surcharge.
  • Nice to have: faster pages.
