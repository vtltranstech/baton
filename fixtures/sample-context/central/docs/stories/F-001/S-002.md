# S-002 Overdue invoices respect the grace period
Feature: F-001
Depends-On: none
Track: spike
State: Draft

## Story
As a Billing Clerk, I want reminders to wait for the grace period so that customers are not chased too early.

## Acceptance criteria
- AC1: Given an invoice in the invoices collection whose due date passed less than the Grace Period ago, when reminders run, then no reminder is sent.
- AC2: Given an Invoice past the Grace Period, when reminders run, then one reminder is sent.
